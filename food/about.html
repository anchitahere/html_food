About FC

Welcome to 'Fry Carts', where we turn the simple art of frying into a crispy, golden obsession. Founded in 2026, we started with a basic mission: to serve the crispiest, most flavorful fried comfort food in 'Kolkata'.
We believe that great fried food is a craft. That's why we use fresh, never-frozen ingredients, hand-batter every item to order, and use a signature blend of spices that keeps you coming back for more. From our perfectly seasoned chicken to our golden, hand-cut sides, every bite is engineered for the ultimate crunch.
Whether you are stopping by for a quick lunch, grabbing a family feast, or satisfying a late-night craving, we treat you like family. Come on in, grab a napkin (or three), and taste the crunch for yourself.

Where and how Fried items invented ?

ancient Egypt around 2500 BC, where archaeological and historical records show cooks first submerged wheat cakes and other foods in boiling oil.

How Frying Was Invented

  • The Method: Early cooks discovered that immersing food in heated oil or animal fat cooked it quickly while creating a protective, crispy outer crust that sealed in moisture.
  • The Fuel and Fats: Early Mediterranean civilizations relied on precious olive oil or sesame oil, while ancient Asian cultures used animal fats and later plant oils in wok cooking.
